# GoreeCloud Reader — Branding

## Product name

**GoreeCloud Reader**

## Product positioning

**Your private home for books, manga, comics, and audiobooks.**

## Identity principles

- Use the official GoreeCloud name and approved Reader identity assets when available.
- Follow the latest applicable Stable Glaze UI system for typography, surfaces, spacing, interaction, motion, accessibility, and responsive behavior.
- Prefer artwork-led library presentation without allowing artwork to reduce readability or accessibility.
- Clearly distinguish synthetic previews, unavailable features, development state, and verified runtime states.
- Do not display security/privacy/recovery badges as decoration when the underlying Platform-System state is absent or unverified.

## Foundation visual direction

The initial clients use calm translucent surfaces, strong content hierarchy, responsive cover grids/cards, restrained motion, and readable progress treatment. These are adoption-direction choices only; they do not constitute formal Glaze UI consumer acceptance.

## Naming

Use `GoreeCloud Reader` in user-facing product contexts and `goreecloud-reader` for repository/package identifiers where appropriate.

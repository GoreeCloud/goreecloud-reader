# GoreeCloud Reader — Competitive Objectives

Reader should compete on product quality without copying another application's protected implementation or identity.

## Objectives

- **Unified media model:** treat books, manga, comics, and audiobooks as related works/editions rather than disconnected files.
- **Specialized experiences:** provide first-class manga/comic, EPUB/PDF, webtoon, and audiobook interfaces.
- **Self-hosted control:** keep personal libraries and activity within the user's authorized GoreeCloud environment by default.
- **Original-file preservation:** avoid destructive source-library rewriting and keep generated resources separate.
- **Cross-format continuity:** eventually help users move between ebook and audiobook editions while retaining independent exact progress.
- **Large-library usability:** maintain fast search, filtering, artwork rendering, and background processing as collections grow.
- **Accessibility:** make reading and listening adaptable across text size, contrast, motion, keyboard/touch input, screen readers, and playback speed.
- **Transparent integration:** show only Platform-System protection, privacy, identity, recovery, and synchronization states that can be proven.
- **Open source and portability:** preserve inspectability, modification rights, independent deployment, export, backup, and migration.

## Benchmark categories

Future evaluations should compare Reader against leading self-hosted and mainstream reading experiences for library organization, reader ergonomics, audiobook controls, metadata quality, offline behavior, accessibility, performance, privacy, and device continuity. Comparisons must remain evidence-based and must not turn inspiration into copied branding or implementation.
